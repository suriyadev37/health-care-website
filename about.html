Since 2012

Our Story

Founded in 2012, MediNova began as a small community practice with one goal: to redefine the healthcare experience by making it more accessible, transparent, and patient-focused.

Over the past decade, our dedicated team has grown into a multi-disciplinary facility offering advanced medical treatments, digital diagnostics, and preventive care for thousands of families. Today, MediNova stands as a beacon of excellence — combining medical expertise with cutting-edge technology to deliver comprehensive healthcare you can rely on.
